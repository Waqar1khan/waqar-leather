Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer custom logo and OEM manufacturing?

+
Yes! WL Leather offers full OEM manufacturing with custom logo embossing, debossing, screen printing, and private labeling. We produce products with your brand identity from scratch, including custom packaging, hang tags and labels.

What is the minimum order quantity?

+
We accept orders starting from 1 piece for samples and personal orders. Wholesale pricing begins at 10+ pieces, with the best rates available for orders of 50+ units. Contact us for a custom quote.

Do you ship internationally?

+
Absolutely! We ship to 50+ countries worldwide including USA, UK, Germany, France, UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman, Pakistan, Turkey and many more. We use DHL, FedEx and Aramex with full tracking.

Is the leather 100% genuine?

+
Yes, all our products are made from 100% genuine leather — premium cowhide, sheepskin and goatskin depending on the product. Every item comes with a genuine leather guarantee. We never use synthetic or bonded leather.
